# Favorite Series Showcase

A curated list of some of my favorite TV series, formatted with Markdown elements.

---

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series created by the Duffer Brothers and released on Netflix.

### Main Characters
1. **Eleven** – A girl with psychokinetic powers  
2. **Mike Wheeler** – Loyal friend and natural leader  
3. **Jim Hopper** – The town’s police chief  
4. **Joyce Byers** – Determined mother of Will  
5. **Demogorgon** – A terrifying creature from the Upside Down  

### Seasons Overview

| Season | Year | Main Plot |
|--------|------|-----------|
| 1      | 2016 | Disappearance of Will and discovery of Eleven |
| 2      | 2017 | Shadow Monster threatens Hawkins |
| 3      | 2019 | Starcourt Mall and Russian experiments |
| 4      | 2022 | Vecna emerges from the Upside Down |

## Breaking Bad

**Breaking Bad** is a crime drama series created by Vince Gilligan, following a chemistry teacher turned meth kingpin.

### Main Characters
- **Walter White** – Chemistry teacher turned drug lord  
- **Jesse Pinkman** – Former student and partner  
- **Skyler White** – Walter’s wife  
- **Hank Schrader** – DEA agent and Walter’s brother-in-law  

---

## The Crown

**The Crown** is a historical drama series about the reign of Queen Elizabeth II, created by Peter Morgan.

### Main Characters
- **Queen Elizabeth II** – The monarch  
- **Prince Philip** – Her husband  
- **Princess Margaret** – Her sister  
- **Winston Churchill** – Prime Minister  

---

## The Mandalorian

**The Mandalorian** is a space western set in the Star Wars universe, created by Jon Favreau.

### Main Characters
- **Din Djarin (The Mandalorian)** – A bounty hunter  
- **Grogu (Baby Yoda)** – A mysterious Force-sensitive child  
- **Cara Dune** – Rebel shock trooper  
- **Moff Gideon** – Imperial antagonist
